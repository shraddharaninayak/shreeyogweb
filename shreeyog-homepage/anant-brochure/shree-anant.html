Craftsmanship & Finishes

Comprehensive Specifications

Every material is selected for durability, aesthetic elegance, and long-term peace of mind.

Structure & Masonry

  • R.C.C. earthquake resistant framed structure as per IS specifications.
  • 6" thick external brickwork and 4" internal brick partitions.
  • Sand-faced exterior plaster with weather-shield protective paint.
  • Smooth gypsum / Neeru finish internal plaster for walls.

Flooring & Kitchen

  • Premium 32" x 32" vitrified tiles in all living spaces, bedrooms, and kitchen.
  • Anti-skid designer ceramic tile flooring for terraces and bathrooms.
  • Polished black granite kitchen platform with stainless steel sink (Nirali / equivalent).
  • Glazed ceramic tiles dado up to lintel level in kitchen & bathrooms.
  • Provision for water purifier and exhaust fan connections.

Doors & Windows

  • Attractive laminate main entrance door with brass/SS designer fittings and night latch.
  • Waterproof flush doors for toilets with cylindrical locks.
  • Powder-coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh and safety grills.
  • Natural granite stone window sills on all four sides.

Electrical & Plumbing

  • Concealed copper wiring with premium modular switches (Legrand / Anchor).
  • Dedicated TV, telephone, and broadband internet conduit points.
  • Adequate AC electrical points in master bedrooms.
  • Concealed CPVC/UPVC plumbing with Jaquar or equivalent CP fittings.
  • Designer branded sanitaryware (Cera / Hindware) with hot & cold mixer.

Eco & Campus Amenities

  • Central solar water heating system connected to master bathroom.
  • Rainwater harvesting system to maintain ground water table.
  • Automatic passenger elevator with battery backup system.
  • Rooftop solar provision and energy-efficient LED campus illumination.
  • Basketball practice ring & children's recreational area.

Security & Convenience

  • Video door phone system for each flat for controlled visitor entry.
  • Dedicated 24x7 security cabin at the entrance gate.
  • Inverter wiring backup provision in all residences.
  • Covered and paved car parking with well-demarcated bays.
