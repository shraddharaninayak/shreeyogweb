Architectural Masterpiece

An Address is a Symbol of Status

Shree Bramhand is a limited-edition residence of the township, designed keeping in mind modern-day comfort and luxury. Situated at a prime location with close proximity to the Mumbai-Agra Highway and Nashik-Pune Highway.

A rare perfection in contemporary style and finish are these individually designed 2 & 3 BHK homes. These spacious homes speak of privacy and warmth, overlooking a lushly landscaped terrace.

  • Individually planned 2 & 3 BHK luxury residences with zero wasted space
  • Spacious private sit-outs and balconies offering breathtaking open vistas
  • Grand entrance lobby with secured access control and high-speed automatic lifts
  • Designed in harmony with Vastu principles and natural cross-ventilation

Quality Benchmark

Comprehensive Specifications

Every detail at Shree Bramhand is crafted with branded fittings, durable construction, and thoughtful inclusions to give you an effortless lifestyle from day one.

Security & Common Safety

  • CCTV surveillance throughout common campus.
  • Entrance lobby with security gate and watchman cabin.
  • Watchman room and driver's rest room with dedicated toilet.
  • Good quality of M.S. safety gate for the main entrance door.
  • Separate letter box and custom nameplate for each home.

Power & Vertical Mobility

  • Stretcher lift and one 8-person automatic lift with genset backup.
  • Provision of inverter wiring for the entire home.
  • Provision of E-vehicle charging point in the parking area.
  • Net metering for common light to ensure minimum common light bills.
  • Common TATA Sky connection for the entire building.
  • Provision of Internet fiber optic cable for each home.
  • Provision for A/C conduit in living and bedrooms.

Plumbing & Water Systems

  • Geyser system provided in every toilet.
  • Provision of separate overhead bore water tank for flushing to save NMC water.
  • ISI mark PVC plumbing material and premium Jaquar fittings.
  • Mirror arrangement on basin area and complete bath accessories for each flat.
  • Top terrace with washbasin and water arrangement for small parties or get-togethers.

Kitchen & Storage Craft

  • Kitchen trolley in premium Stainless Steel (SS) make.
  • L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full height dado.
  • Designer Crockery Unit provided with every residence.
  • Shoe rack and cloth dryer stand provided for each home.
  • Entire home enlightened with tube light set, bulbs, fans, Aquaguard, and exhaust fans.

Flooring & Joinery

  • 800 × 800 vitrified tile flooring of well-known brand throughout.
  • Three-track anodized sliding windows with mosquito net.
  • All door shutters of bedroom and main door are laminated on both sides.
  • All toilets and window openings finished with polished granite frames.
  • Each toilet provided with full-height designer ceramic dado.

Finishes & Structure

  • Masonry work completed with superior eco-light blocks for thermal insulation.
  • Provision of aesthetic false ceiling in the living hall.
  • Curtain rods with curtains provided for the entire home.
  • Internal coloring in Asian Paints Tractor Emulsion.
  • External paint in Asian Paints Apex weather protect exterior finish.
  • Separate demarcated parking slot for each home.
