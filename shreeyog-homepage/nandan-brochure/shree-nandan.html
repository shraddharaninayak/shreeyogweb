Quality Materials & Fittings

26 Comprehensive Specifications from Brochure

Shree Nandan is executed with precision materials, branded sanitary ware, robust electrical protections, and ready-to-use fixtures for hassle-free living.

Structure & Masonry

  • Brick Work: Done in natural red soil brick or Eco light blocks for superior thermal insulation.
  • Internal Painting: Finished in smooth Tractor Emulsion for enduring elegance.
  • External Painting: Asian Paints Apex Weather Protect high-performance exterior coating.
  • Doors: All bedroom and main door shutters laminated on both sides with high quality.
  • Security Gate: Good quality M.S. security gate provided for the main entrance door.
  • Door & Window Frames: All toilets and windows designed with solid granite frames.

Kitchen & Utility

  • Kitchen Platform: L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full height wall dado.
  • SS Kitchen Trolleys: Stainless steel trolleys with dedicated crockery storage unit.
  • Dryer Stand: Heavy-duty cloth dryer stand provided for each home in the utility area.
  • Water Purifier: Aquaguard system installed and ready in every kitchen.
  • Top Terrace Sink: Dedicated basin with water arrangement on terrace for get-togethers.

Flooring & Sanitary

  • Flooring: 800 × 800 mm premium vitrified tiles of reputed brand across all rooms.
  • Toilet Wall Dado: Full height ceramic designer wall dado in each bathroom.
  • Fittings: Genuine Jaquar CP fittings for long-lasting durability.
  • Plumbing Material: ISI marked high-grade PVC pipes and plumbing fixtures.
  • Hot Water Geyser: Dedicated geyser system installed in every toilet.
  • Bath Accessories: Mirror arrangement at wash basin area and full bathroom accessories.

Electrical, Elevators & Safety

  • Net Metering: Solar net metering for common lighting — no common lighting electricity bill!
  • Inverter Provision: Pre-wired conduit and cabling for full-home inverter backup.
  • A/C Conduit: Pre-installed air conditioner electrical and drainage conduits.
  • Passenger Lift: 6-person automatic passenger lift with generator (Genset) backup.
  • Surveillance: 24/7 CCTV surveillance cameras covering entry, lobby, and parking.
  • Move-in Ready: Full home enlightened with tube light sets, LED bulbs, ceiling fans, shoe rack, and curtain rods.
  • Common Amenities: Entrance lobby safety gate, watchman room with toilet, separate nameplate & letter box, and common TATA Sky dish connection.
