  • Brickwork done with natural red soil brick or Eco light blocks.

Kitchen & Utility

  • L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full wall dado.
  • SS kitchen trolleys with dedicated crockery storage unit.
  • Aquaguard water purifier installed and ready for use.
  • Cloth dryer stand provided for utility area in each home.

Flooring & Bathrooms

  • 800 x 800 mm premium vitrified flooring of well-known brand.
  • Full ceramic wall dado tiles up to lintel in every toilet.
  • ISI mark PVC plumbing materials and Jaguar CP bath fittings.
  • Geyser system in every toilet + wash basin mirror and accessories.

Electrical & Solar

  • Net metering for common light (Zero common light bill).
  • Full-home inverter wiring provision for uninterrupted power.
  • Dedicated A/C conduit provision for clean installation.
  • Common TATA Sky connection + tube light sets, bulbs & fans included.

Safety & Elevators

  • Automatic 6-passenger elevators with dedicated battery backup.
  • Comprehensive 24/7 CCTV surveillance across perimeter & lobbies.
  • Entrance lobby with safety gate and watchman cabin with toilet.
  • Separate allocated parking, custom letter box & nameplate per home.
