Lifestyle Concept

Designed for Generations, Crafted for Life

Shreeyog Builders & Developers conceptualized Shree Niwas around the idea that home should be a sanctuary of health, community, and tranquility. Every residence is positioned to optimize privacy while offering family-friendly campus features right within the gates.

From the dedicated outdoor green gym and senior citizens' sit-out enclave to the vibrant children's play area, Shree Niwas nurtures healthy family living for every generation.

Amenities & Craftsmanship

Campus Features & Specifications

Curated materials, trusted fittings, and lifestyle amenities engineered for enduring everyday comfort.

Campus Lifestyle Amenities

  • Children's play area with safe outdoor recreational equipment.
  • Dedicated senior citizens' sit-out enclave with pergola shading.
  • Open-air green gym stations for healthy fitness routines.
  • Grand entrance gate with security cabin and CCTV surveillance.
  • Automatic passenger lift with battery backup emergency landing.

Structure & Walls

  • Earthquake-resistant R.C.C. framed structure engineered to IS seismic norms.
  • 6" external brickwork with double-coat sand-faced plaster.
  • Internal smooth gypsum/neeru finish plaster with acrylic washable paint.
  • Waterproof treatment on all terraces and toilet slabs.

Flooring & Kitchen

  • Premium 32" x 32" vitrified tiles in all rooms.
  • Anti-skid designer tiles in bathrooms and attached terraces.
  • Polished black granite kitchen counter with stainless steel sink.
  • Glazed tiles dado up to lintel level in kitchen and bathrooms.
  • Provisions for water purifier, exhaust fan, and washing machine.

Electrical & Plumbing

  • Concealed copper wiring with modular switches (Anchor / Legrand).
  • Adequate light, fan, TV, telephone, and inverter points.
  • AC points provided in bedrooms.
  • Concealed CPVC/UPVC plumbing with branded Jaguar / equivalent CP fittings.
  • Designer branded sanitaryware with hot & cold water mixer.

Doors & Windows

  • Decorative main door with attractive laminate, night latch, and quality handles.
  • Waterproof flush doors for toilets with cylindrical locks.
  • Powder coated aluminum sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
  • Granite stone window sills on all four sides with safety grills.

Eco & Safety Systems

  • Solar water heating system with hot water point to bathroom.
  • Rainwater harvesting recharge pits for sustainable conservation.
  • Video door phone installed in every flat for verified visitor access.
  • Firefighting protection system as per fire safety norms.
