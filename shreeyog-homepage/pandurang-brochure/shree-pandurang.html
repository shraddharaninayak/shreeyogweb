Uncompromising Quality

Complete Technical Specifications

Over 25 carefully specified construction and lifestyle elements verified directly from page 2 of the official Shree Pandurang brochure.

Structure & Masonry

  • Earthquake-resistant RCC framed structure designed by Er. Bhavesh Patel.
  • Brickwork in natural red soil bricks or eco-light block.
  • Staircase, landing & lift area excluded from built-up calculation.
  • Asian Paints Apex weather-protect external paint finish.
  • Internal tractor emulsion paint finish for all walls.

Doors & Windows

  • Good quality M.S. security gate for main entrance door.
  • All door shutters of bedrooms & main door with both sides laminate.
  • Door frames of box-type in plywood with both sides laminate.
  • All windows fitted with premium granite framing.
  • Curtain rods with curtains provided for entire home.

Flooring & Wall Tiling

  • Premium flooring & wall tiles of Johnson brand or RAK ceramics.
  • Full height wall tiles for all bathrooms and toilets.
  • All toilet door openings crafted with elegant granite frames.
  • Mirror arrangement in wash basin area & bath accessories included.
  • Anti-skid ceramic flooring for all attached balconies.

Kitchen & Utility

  • Parallel granite kitchen platform with full height ceramic dado.
  • Pre-installed modern kitchen trolley with crockery unit.
  • Dedicated dry utility balcony with washing machine tap points.
  • Foldable cloth dryer stand installed for every residence.
  • Aqua Guard water purifier pre-installed in kitchen.

Electrical & Smart Tech

  • Net metering for common lighting (Zero common lighting electricity bill).
  • Provision for complete inverter wiring throughout entire home.
  • Air conditioner (A/C) conduit provisions in bedrooms.
  • Common TATA Sky with high-speed Wi-Fi cabling.
  • CCTV surveillance covering entrance lobby & parking area.

Plumbing, Lift & Welcome Kit

  • ISI-marked PVC plumbing materials & sanitary fittings.
  • Geyser system pre-installed in every bathroom.
  • Automatic 6-person elevator with battery backup.
  • Provision of designer false ceiling in living room.
  • Complimentary move-in set: tube lights, ceiling fans, doormats, bucket, dustbin, supli, bath stools, key holder, and separate nameplate & letter box.
