Uncompromising Quality

Complete Technical Specifications

Over 25 carefully specified construction and lifestyle elements verified directly from page 15 of the official Shree Safalya brochure.

Structure & Masonry

  • Seismic-resistant RCC framed structure designed by Er. Shailesh Dhumane.
  • Masonry work executed in high-efficiency eco-light blocks.
  • 11-foot clear ceiling height throughout all residential levels.
  • Asian Paints Apex weather-protect external paint finish.
  • Internal Tractor Emulsion paint finish for smooth, elegant walls.

Doors & Windows

  • Good quality heavy M.S. security gate for main entrance door.
  • Door frames of box-type in plywood with laminate on both sides.
  • All bedroom and main door shutters laminated on both sides.
  • All window frames finished in premium polished granite.
  • Curtain rods provided throughout entire residence.

Flooring & Wall Tiling

  • Flooring & wall tiles of Johnson brand or RAK ceramics.
  • Full height designer wall tiles in each toilet.
  • All toilet openings and bathroom sills crafted with granite frames.
  • Mirror arrangement in wash basin area & bath accessories included.
  • Anti-skid flooring for all attached balconies and utility areas.

Kitchen & Utility

  • Parallel granite kitchen platform with full height ceramic dado.
  • Modular kitchen trolley with crockery unit pre-installed.
  • Dedicated dry utility balcony with washing machine tap points.
  • Foldable cloth dryer stand provided for every residence.
  • Aqua Guard water purifier pre-installed in kitchen.

Electrical & Smart Tech

  • Net metering for common lighting (Zero common lighting electricity bill).
  • Provision for complete inverter wiring throughout entire home.
  • Air conditioner (A/C) conduit provisions in all bedrooms and living hall.
  • Common TATA Sky connection with high-speed Wi-Fi cabling.
  • CCTV surveillance covering lobby, parking bays & perimeter gates.

Elevators, Parking & Fixtures

  • Two automatic 14 to 16 passenger stretcher elevators with battery backup.
  • Dedicated two car parking spaces for each residence.
  • Geyser system pre-installed in every bathroom.
  • Fire-fighting sprinkler and hydrant infrastructure.
  • Move-in kit: tube lights, ceiling fans, doormats, dustbin, bath accessories, key holder, and separate nameplate & letter box.
