QUALITY STANDARDS

Comprehensive Specifications & Features

Every specification is crafted using tested materials, branded fittings, and sustainable green energy provisions.

Civil & Architectural Specifications

  • Masonry & Brickwork: Natural red soil brick or Eco light blocks ensuring durability and thermal insulation.
  • Kitchen Platform: L-shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full-height designer glazed tiles dado.
  • Doors & Shutters: All bedroom and main door shutters laminated on both sides with high-quality brass/SS hardware.
  • Windows & Frames: 3-track anodized sliding windows with mosquito nets. All toilets and windows finished with granite framing.
  • Security Gate: Heavy-gauge, aesthetic M.S. safety gate installed for main entrance door of each flat.
  • Bathrooms & Toilets: Full-height glazed dado tiles with anti-skid floor ceramic. Geyser system provision in every toilet.
  • Flooring: Premium 800 mm × 800 mm double-charged vitrified flooring of reputed brand across all living spaces.
  • Plumbing & Sanitary: ISI-mark PVC plumbing conduits with branded Jaquar bathroom fittings and accessories.

Electrical, Amenities & Green Energy

  • Solar Net Metering: Rooftop solar system with net metering for common lighting — zero common electric charges.
  • Elevator: Automatic 6-person passenger lift with generator/genset power backup.
  • Electrical Wiring: Concealed copper wiring with inverter backup wiring for the entire home, plus A/C conduits.
  • Living Room Decor: Designer false ceiling provision in drawing hall with ambient LED cove arrangement.
  • Painting: Internal walls treated with tractor emulsion; exterior finished with Asian Paints Apex weather protect.
  • Kitchen Accessories: Modular stainless steel (SS) kitchen trolley set fitted.
  • Smart Additions: Entire home equipped with tube light set, ceiling fans, Aquaguard water purifier, and shoe rack.
  • Security & Parking: Dedicated parking bay per home, CCTV surveillance system, watchman room with toilet, and security entrance lobby.
