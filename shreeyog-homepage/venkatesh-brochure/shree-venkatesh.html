Uncompromising Craftsmanship

29 Authentic Technical Specifications

Every component at Shree Venkatesh Park has been engineered for enduring durability, hassle-free maintenance, and premium lifestyle convenience.

Structure & Flooring
  • Brick work done in natural red soil brick or Eco light blocks.
  • Flooring 800mm x 800mm vitrified tiles of well known brand.
  • Internal colouring with Asian Paints Tractor Emulsion.
  • External paint with Asian Paints Apex weather protect.
  • Entrance lobby with safety gate.
Modular Kitchen & Utility
  • L shape or parallel granite kitchen platform with full dado.
  • Kitchen trolley in SS (Stainless Steel) make.
  • Aquaguard water purifier included in kitchen.
  • Dedicated cloth dryer stand provided for each home.
  • Separate washing & utility terrace area.
Bathrooms & Plumbing
  • Each toilet provided with full height glazed ceramic dado.
  • Geyser system installed in every toilet.
  • ISI mark PVC plumbing material and Jaquar fittings.
  • All toilets and windows framed with polished granite.
  • Mirror arrangement on basin area with bath accessories for each flat.
  • Separate overhead water tank for flushing to preserve NMC drinking water.
Doors, Windows & Security
  • Good quality of M.S. security gate for main entrance door.
  • All door shutters of bedroom and main door are laminated on both sides.
  • 3 Track anodised sliding windows with mosquito net.
  • CCTV surveillance across all strategic building zones.
  • Watchman room with attached toilet.
Electrical & Move-In Setup
  • Provision for inverter wiring for the entire home.
  • Provision for A/C copper piping & electrical conduit.
  • Provision of decorative false ceiling in living hall.
  • Entire home enlightened with tube light set, designer bulbs, fan, shoe rack, etc.
  • Curtain rods with curtains provided for entire home.
Building Infrastructure
  • Automatic 6 person lift with dedicated genset power backup.
  • Net metering for common light (zero common electricity bill).
  • Separate allocated car parking for each residence.
  • Separate personalized letter box and nameplate for each home.
  • Common Tata Sky satellite connection for the building.
  • Top terrace with wash basin and water arrangement for gatherings.
