Vision & Heritage

A City with Spirituality.. Cool Climate.. Becoming a Smart City.

Nashik in the modern age has transformed dynamically across agriculture, industrialisation, construction, and education to mark its presence on the international map. The city boasts abundant water, round-the-clock power, and superior arterial roads to effortlessly handle rising transit.

With its cozy, pleasant climate and lush green landscape, Nashik stands as one of India's most sacred and culturally profound destinations — situated merely 185 km from Mumbai, 200 km from Pune, and 80 km from the sacred shrine of Shri Saibaba's Shirdi. Known as Maharashtra's fastest-developing city and 'Tomorrow’s Metro', business leaders and homeowners alike are choosing Nashik for enduring lifestyle excellence.

“Shreeyog Builders & Developers was established with a singular vision: to redefine property development in Nashik. With over 25 years of concrete expertise across Indira Nagar, Gangapur Road, Govind Nagar, Deepali Nagar, Bhabha Nagar, Bodhale Nagar & Rajiv Nagar, our landmarks stand for quality, transparency, and enduring trust.”

Civil & Finishing Standards

Amenities & Technical Specifications

Every detail is executed using the highest engineering standards, reputable branded materials, and stringent quality control protocols.

R.C.C. Structure

Earthquake resistant R.C.C. frame structure engineered for supreme seismic durability.

Brick Work & Masonry

External walls 6" thick masonry and internal dividing walls 4.5" thick brick work.

Plaster & Paint

Double coat external plaster with semi Acrylic paint; Neeru finish internal plaster with ASIAN Oil Bound Distemper.

Flooring

Premium 2' × 2' Vitrified flooring (Johnson or equivalent branded) throughout the entire apartment.

Kitchen Platform

Granite L-shape counter or Granite kitchen & service platform (G20) with stainless steel sink & full glazed tile dado.

Windows & Grills

3-track powder coated aluminium sliding windows with mosquito net, protective M.S. safety grills & marble sill framing.

Doors & Fittings

Main entrance door with brass fittings and security night latch; waterproof flush doors with both-side laminates for internal rooms.

Bath, Toilet & Plumbing

Full height glazed wall tiles; laminate shutters with granite frames; concealed plumbing with branded C.P. fittings & wall mixer.

Electrification & Parking

Concealed fire-resistant electrical wiring throughout; separate designated parking bay reserved for each flat.

Communication & Water Supply

Telephone & cable TV points in living zone; round-the-clock water supply from Municipal Corporation & borewell backup.
